Energy Flux Analytical Validation Note

This page summarizes the analytical checks of the energy solve with the Robin surface boundary (see Energy Balance). They are executed in test/test_longwave_consistency.jl and test/test_case_api.jl.

Insulated Equilibrium

With all surface fluxes and emission switched off and a uniform temperature, the solve must leave interface and layer temperatures unchanged and diagnose no melt. This is checked for a regular (100 kg m⁻²) and a vanishingly thin (10⁻⁸ kg m⁻²) surface layer: eliminating the interface temperature must not create a numerically singular top row, so the diagonal stays exactly one.

Graybody Radiative Equilibrium

A surface at T0 under incident longwave $\sigma T_0^4$ from an isothermal blackbody environment absorbs and emits the same graybody flux regardless of emissivity, so neither snow nor bare ice may warm or melt. The original BESSI treatment (seb_scheme=:bessi, unit incident absorption) is checked to retain its spurious net gain $(1-\epsilon)\,\sigma T_0^4$.

Interface Conductance

For two layers under pure diffusion, the heat exchanged between them must follow the harmonic interface conductance $G_{i+1/2} = 2K_iK_{i+1}/(K_{i+1}\Delta z_i + K_i\Delta z_{i+1})$.

Melting Boundary

When the surface energy balance would raise the interface above T0:

  • melt is diagnosed even if the centre of the top cell is still below T0;
  • the conduction $G_s(T_0 - T_1)$ from the melting surface into the snow below is retained, and the melt energy is the remainder of the surface energy balance;
  • the sensible-energy change of the column plus the melt energy equals the energy supplied at the surface.

Ice Substrate

On a bare-ice column the public go_energy_flux! solves the ice substrate: a negative surface energy balance cools both the interface and the top substrate layer below T0 instead of being discarded.
